منبع (R1 تا R6)، نحوهٔ حل آن‌ها، و مواردی که هنوز نیاز به تأیید نهایی دارند. انتشار

This document records the fact-checking process behind the guide: conflicts
between the six source research reports (R1–R6), how each was resolved, and
open items still needing final confirmation. Publishing these notes matches
the guide's own rule — no number appears without a source.

# Editor's Notes (English)

**Conflicts between reports and how they were resolved**

1. **R4's citations are unresolved.** R4's source tags (【turn0searchN】) do not resolve to URLs in the delivered file. Every R4-unique claim in this guide (consulting, patent/IP careers, science-policy, AI-lab employers abroad, ILSF, postdoc-بورس details beyond IPM/Sharif, the "salary cap removed" directive, the ~65M toman assistant-professor starting salary, faculty salary protests) therefore carries reduced confidence and should be re-verified or re-sourced before publication. Where R1 offers a sourced alternative, R1 was preferred.
2. **Faculty salary (Iran).** R1 (Mehr via Heiatelmi, Apr 2024): cap 55M toman for full professor; ministry proposal 65/70/75M. R4: "cap removed," assistant professor ~65M (late 2024/early 2025). Both presented as scale caps, not medians; R1 preferred as better-sourced. **Needs checking with the faculty's HR.**
3. **Conscription duration.** R1: ordinary service now 14 months (Guardian Council announcement, Esfand 1402), home-city posting 21 months, امریه 24 months. R2: "24→21 months cut (Mehrdad 1403 decision)" with 14–21-month range across sources; one R2 source reverses the 14/21 assignment. The guide states "14–21 months depending on location/type" and flags the discrepancy; امریه = 24 months is consistent across R1/R2/R3. Verify current numbers with نظام وظیفه.
4. **KB-امریه GPA criterion.** R1 (Avije, updated 3 Tir 1405): GPA is *not* a criterion — only the points system (≥80 points; 7/month insured work + 5 per 3bn-rial turnover). R2 (JobVision/fanbonyan): minimum GPA 14. R3 (irantahsil): GPA ≥15 (MSc)/≥16 (PhD) for MSRT-track امریه. The guide presents the points system as the core mechanism and notes the inconsistency; the MSRT (university-track) GPA thresholds are separate from the KB-company track.
5. **سرباز نخبه mechanics.** R1: 14–21 months including training. R2: کسر خدمت of 3–19 months; relaunched خرداد ۱۴۰۵; 2,720 applications for 1,000 places (Tabnak, مرداد ۱۴۰۵). The guide presents both framings with the demand datum.
6. **امریه duration for PhDs.** R4 says service "typically 2 years but can be reduced based on education level"; R1/R2/R3 do not support education-based reduction of امریه (reductions run through the نخبگان project route instead). R1/R2/R3 preferred.
7. **US visa-related numbers.** R1 attributes "37.6% FY2025" to *US visitor visas* for Iranians; R5 attributes the same figure to *Canada student-visa approval* (GetVisaOdds, whose page title concerns Canada). The guide uses 37.6% for Canada student approval (R5's reading) — R1's attribution likely a mislabel. US student-visa figures (13 issued Jun–Sep 2025 vs ~1,300 in 2024) are from R5/NIAC and consistent with R3.
8. **Proclamation dates.** R3 says the suspension proclamation is "9 June 2025"; R5 names Proclamation 10949 (June 4, 2025) plus 10998 (Dec 16, 2025). Signed-vs-effective dates plausibly explain the gap; the guide says "خرداد ۲۰۲۵" without over-specifying.
9. **TOEFL/GRE halt date.** R1/R3: halt in 2025 (after OFAC suspended General License G). R5 describes the suspension as a June/August 2026 action. The guide presents it as a 2025 halt with further tightening through 2026; exact month/sequence should be re-verified against ETS's page.
10. **BLS physicist median.** R2 cites $172,250 (May 2025 OOH); R1 cites $137,380 (2024, via Research.com). Both kept with dates; they reflect different vintages of the same OOH series, not a contradiction.
11. **Data-analyst pay in Iran.** R1 (JobVision 1403, ~36M, MSc-level Tehran), R3 (IranTalent 1403, 22.4M average), R2 (IranTalent/Iransalary 1404, median band 30–57.3M). These are different sources, years and category definitions; all kept with dates. No BSc-specific figure exists.
12. **Student counts abroad.** R1 quotes ">110,000 Iranians abroad (2024); US 12,430" via IIE/Open Doors fact-sheet PDF that was bot-protected at access time (data used via Persian summaries). Re-verify against IIE before print.
